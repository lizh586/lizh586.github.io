## Why a Personal Website

As a 研0 student transitioning from mathematics to computer science, I wanted a central place to document my learning journey, showcase projects, and track my LeetCode progress — all while keeping a professional online presence for internship applications.

## Tech Stack

This site is built with:

- **Hugo** — fast static site generator
- **Coder theme** — clean, minimal design
- **GitHub Pages** — free hosting with automatic deployment

The entire site is written in Markdown, making it easy to update content without touching HTML.
